Double Tap

Tap your thumb and index finger together twice to switch the main metric while you run.

PACE⇄AVG PACE

Switch between current and average pace for free. All Metrics adds lap pace and, outdoors, elevation gain.

Requires an Apple Watch that supports Double Tap, with Runner’s Cycle main metrics setting turned on.

Your layout

The main metric gets the most space, with heart rate above and distance and time below.

Choose your main metric

Current pace, average pace, or heart rate. Tap the main value to switch during a run.

Customize colors and text

Pick metrics, colors, and text weights on the watch or in the iPhone app. Hide the top row for a larger main value.

Syncs with your iPhone

Your layout syncs between iPhone and Apple Watch. Runner records the run on the watch, so you can leave the phone at home.

Outdoor and indoor

Outdoor

Records your run with GPS. After the run, see your route colored by relative pace. The workout and route are saved to Apple Health.

Indoor

Uses the watch’s motion sensors for distance and pace. No GPS or route recording.

Hold OUTDOOR or INDOOR on the start screen to switch modes.

Metrics

Workout recording and the core metrics are free. All Metrics is an optional one-time purchase in the iPhone app.
