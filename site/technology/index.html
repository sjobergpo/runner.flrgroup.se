How Runner works.

From the sensors on your wrist to the workout saved in Apple Health.

From Apple Watch to Apple Health

  1. Apple Watch

    Your watch measures heart rate and motion, and uses GPS on outdoor runs.

  2. Apple’s frameworks

    HealthKit provides workout measurements. Core Location provides outdoor GPS and altitude.

  3. Runner

    Runner puts the metrics on your wrist and calculates average pace, lap pace, and elevation gain.

  4. Apple Health

    With your permission, Runner saves your workout and any available route to Apple Health.

Where the numbers come from

Metric sources and calculations
Outdoor pace
Apple’s running speed from HealthKit, converted to minutes per kilometre or mile.
Distance
HealthKit is the primary source. Outdoors, Runner uses filtered GPS distance as a fallback until HealthKit provides distance.
Heart rate & active time
Heart rate comes from HealthKit. Active time comes from Apple’s workout builder and excludes pauses.
Average pace
Runner calculates pace from distance and active time, smoothing the live display. The final summary uses the total distance and active time without that smoothing.
Lap pace
Runner calculates pace for automatic distance laps, using distance and active time and estimating when each lap boundary was crossed.
Elevation gain
Runner calculates climbs from filtered altitude readings supplied by Core Location. Available outdoors only.
Indoor pace
HealthKit supplies motion-based distance. Runner calculates current pace from changes in that distance over a short window of active time. No GPS is used.
Heart rate zones
Zone boundaries and time in each zone come from HealthKit on watchOS 27 or later. Runner uses your current heart rate to show your position within those zones.

Runner filters poor-quality GPS positions and implausible jumps. Missing or outdated heart rate and current pace readings appear as a dash.

Apple documentation includes workout sessions and Core Location.

Your data, your choice.

Runner processes your workout on Apple Watch. We receive no health or location data. Read the privacy policy.
