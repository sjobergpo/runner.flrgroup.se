01 / THE APP

Workout and location data.

Runner uses Apple HealthKit to run workout sessions and obtain available measurements such as heart rate, running speed, walking and running distance, active energy, and workout time. With your permission, workouts and associated available measurements are saved to Apple Health.

For outdoor runs, Runner uses location on Apple Watch to record a GPS route and estimate distance and elevation gain. Available routes are associated with the saved HealthKit workout. Indoor runs use the watch’s motion-based distance measurements and do not record GPS routes or elevation gain.

Runner processes these health and location measurements on the watch. It does not transmit them to FLR Group, an analytics provider, or a developer-operated server. Unless you connect Strava, the Runner iPhone app does not read them either; see Strava.

Apple Health may sync or share saved information according to your Apple settings and the permissions you grant to other apps. Those Apple-managed features are outside Runner’s own data transfers. See Apple’s Health privacy information.

02 / STORAGE & PURCHASES

What stays on your devices.

Settings and recovery

Runner stores layout choices, colors, text weights, workout preferences, and cached purchase-access state locally. During a workout, it also keeps local state needed to try to reconnect to an interrupted session, including relevant progress for lap and elevation calculations. This is not a separate backup or export of a workout.

The paired iPhone and Apple Watch exchange layout settings and verified purchase-access state through Apple’s WatchConnectivity. Health measurements and GPS routes are not included in that settings sync.

All Metrics

The optional All Metrics purchase and restoration are handled by Apple’s App Store. Runner checks Apple-verified entitlements on iPhone and sends the resulting access status to the paired watch. FLR Group does not receive your payment-card details through Runner and does not operate a purchase-verification server. Apple processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

No advertising or analytics

Runner requires no account of its own and includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not collect, sell, or use your workout data for advertising.

03 / STRAVA

If you connect Strava.

The Strava connection is being introduced gradually and may not yet be available in your version of Runner.

Connecting Strava is optional and off until you choose it in the Runner iPhone app. Once connected, Runner uploads your runs to your own Strava account, automatically or when you tap Sync, depending on your setting.

What is sent

For each run that ends after you connect, the Runner iPhone app reads that workout from Apple Health, with your permission, and uploads it directly to Strava: start and end time, distance, heart rate, and for outdoor runs the GPS route. Indoor runs are marked as indoor and have no route. Runs recorded before you connected are never sent. Strava processes uploaded activities under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sign-in and access

You sign in on Strava’s own page. Runner asks only for permission to upload activities. It does not read your Strava activities or other athletes’ data; the only information it receives from Strava is your name, shown as “Connected as”. Strava’s access tokens are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain, on that device only.

Token service

Strava requires a confidential app credential when you sign in, renew access, or disconnect. To keep that credential out of the app, those three requests pass through a small service FLR Group runs on Cloudflare Workers. It adds the credential, forwards the request to Strava, and returns Strava’s answer. It does not store your tokens, and your workouts never pass through it.

To make sure requests come from Runner on a genuine iPhone, the service uses Apple’s App Attest. It stores one public key and a counter for each installation of the app. That record contains no name, account, Strava identity, or workout data.

The service keeps a technical log for three days with the request type, the result, and how long it took. It does not log IP addresses, tokens, or anything that identifies you. Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver each request; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Disconnecting

Disconnect in the Runner iPhone app under Settings → Strava. Runner then revokes its access at Strava and deletes the tokens and its list of synced runs from your iPhone. You can also remove Runner on Strava’s website under Settings → My Apps; Runner then disconnects the next time it contacts Strava. Activities already uploaded stay on Strava until you delete them there.

04 / YOUR CHOICES

You control access.

You can change Runner’s Health and location permissions in your devices’ Health and Privacy & Security settings. Revoking a permission can limit the related feature or leave some metrics unavailable. Runner needs permission to save workouts to Apple Health before starting a workout.

Retention and deletion

Saved workouts and routes remain in Apple Health until you delete them using Apple’s Health or Fitness controls. Uninstalling Runner does not delete workouts already saved to Health.

Runner’s preferences and recovery state are kept in its local app storage as needed for those features. Removing Runner removes its local app storage on that device; remove it from both devices to remove both copies. Apple-managed backups, Health syncing, and App Store purchase history follow your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. There is no Runner server account or developer-held workout database to delete. If you use Strava, its data is managed in your Strava account; see Strava.

05 / WEBSITE & SUPPORT

When you visit or write.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, browser storage, or third-party font services. Page assets are served from this site.

GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages websites. This hosting activity is separate from Runner’s on-device workout processing. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support email

If you email us, FLR Group receives your sender address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and resolve your request, and retain it only as needed to handle the correspondence and any applicable obligations. Email is handled outside the Runner app through email providers.

Please avoid sending health measurements, GPS routes, or other sensitive information unless needed for an issue you choose to discuss. You may ask us about or request deletion of your support correspondence using the address below.

External links

Links to Apple, GitHub, Strava, Cloudflare, and FLR Group open services governed by their own privacy policies. Runner does not embed their analytics or purchase forms on this website.
